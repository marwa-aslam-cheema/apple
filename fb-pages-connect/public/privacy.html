Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

This app ("Page Direct") is operated by {{BUSINESS_NAME}} to manage its own Facebook Pages and to send order, booking and account updates to customers who have contacted those Pages. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we receive from Facebook

  • The name and Facebook ID of the Page administrator who logs in.
  • The list of Pages that administrator manages, and the access token for each Page.
  • For each Page: the names and Page-scoped IDs of people who have messaged the Page, the time and a short snippet of their latest message, and the content of conversations that the administrator opens in the inbox.

How we use it

  • To show the administrator their Pages, customers and conversations.
  • To send replies and order, booking or account updates to people who contacted the Page, using Facebook Message Tags as allowed by the Messenger Platform policy.
  • To keep a log of messages sent, so failures can be investigated.

We do not send promotional messages with Message Tags. Every update ends with an instruction to reply STOP, and anyone who replies STOP is not messaged again.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell this data or share it with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Facebook's own servers (Meta Graph API) to perform the actions above.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on the server where the app runs, and is accessible only to the Page administrator. It is kept until the administrator disconnects the app or asks for deletion.

Deleting your data

You can remove the app from your Facebook settings (Settings > Business Integrations), which triggers automatic deletion, or follow the steps on our data deletion page. Customers can also reply STOP to any message, or contact us to have their data removed.
