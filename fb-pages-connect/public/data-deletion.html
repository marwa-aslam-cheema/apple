Data Deletion Instructions

You can have your data removed from "Page Direct" in either of these ways:

  1. From Facebook (automatic): go to Facebook > Settings & privacy > Settings > Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), find "Page Direct" and choose Remove. Facebook then notifies us and we delete the stored Page connections, access tokens, customer records and message logs for your account.
  2. By email: write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the name of the Page and the Facebook name you used. We will delete the data and confirm within 30 days.

If you are a customer of a Page that uses this app and want your records removed, reply STOP to any message, or email the address above.
